# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors, maintainers, and community members pledge to create a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to fostering an open and respectful community where collaboration, learning, and innovation can thrive.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Treating everyone with respect, empathy, and kindness
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Providing constructive feedback gracefully
- Being supportive of fellow contributors and beginners
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Demonstrating professionalism in discussions and collaborations

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful speech
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Public or private intimidation
- Sharing others’ private information without consent
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable standards of behavior. Maintainers have the authority to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, social platforms, and any other community interactions representing the project.

---

## Reporting Guidelines

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers through the appropriate communication channels. All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially and fairly.

---

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may result in warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent removal from the community, depending on the severity of the violation.
